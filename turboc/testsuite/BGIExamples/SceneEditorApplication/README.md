# SceneEditorApplication (BGI, real mode DOS)

The menu-only Scene Editor of the cpp minimal kernel (`cpp/x86_64_smp_kernel/src/main/programs/sceneEditor`) on the BGI backend (`turboc/bgi`): it reads the menu bar of `etc/gui/english.json` (or `spanish.json`, or another file) and lets the user browse it with the mouse and the keyboard (F10, arrows, Enter, Escape, mnemonics); chosen items are reported, not executed. `r` and `R` step through the graphics modes with 1, 4, 8, 16 and 24 bits per pixel that the machine can set; Escape closes a menu, then quits.

Build it in DOS after mapping the tree to 8.3 names and building, in this order, `BASE\BASE86.BAT`, `BASE\BASELK86.BAT`, `BGI\BGI.BAT` and `BGI\BGILINK.BAT`; then, from `TESTSUIT\BGIEXAM\SCENEDIT`, `COMPILE.BAT` builds `SCENEDIT.EXE` and `RUN.BAT` runs it (`RUN spanish`, `RUN english G800x600x64K`, `SCENEDIT -h` lists the modes). The BGI drivers are read from `C:\TURBOC\BGI` (or `BGIPATH`): `EGAVGA.BGI` and `CGA.BGI` of Turbo C++ and the SvgaBGI drivers `SVGA16.BGI`, `SVGA256.BGI`, `SVGA64K.BGI` and `SVGATC.BGI`. A mouse driver (`MOUSE.COM`, CuteMouse, ...) is optional.

Tested in VirtualBox (VBE adapter, MS-DOS with `MOUSE.COM`): `r` and `R` step through 15 modes, the CGA and monochrome ones, 640x350 and 640x480 with 16 colours, 800x600 to 1280x1024 with 16 colours (`SVGA16`) and 320x200 to 1280x1024 with 256 colours (`SVGA256`), with the menus opening and closing in each one. `SVGA64K.BGI` and `SVGATC.BGI` are built with `USEVESA EQU 0` and only know chipset specific modes, so VirtualBox offers no 16 or 24 bit mode through them; rebuilt with `USEVESA EQU 1` (`MAKEBGI.BAT` of SvgaBGI) they would go through the VBE.
